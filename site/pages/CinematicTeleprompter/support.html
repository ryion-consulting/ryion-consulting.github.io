The questions asked most

I subscribed or bought Lifetime Unlock, but a device still asks me to pay
Open Settings → Restore Purchases on that device, signed in to the same Apple Account you used. One plan or purchase covers all your iPhones and iPads, and nothing is charged twice.
How do I cancel my subscription?
In the app, open Settings → Manage Subscription. You can also use Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends, and you keep everything until then.
The recorder says "Camera Access Is Off"
Tap Open Settings and allow the camera. The same goes for the microphone: without it, takes are recorded with no sound. If the app says Another app is using the camera or microphone, close that app, or end the call, and try again.
Voice-Follow doesn't move
It needs the microphone, and the script's language must match what you're reading: tap the globe under the script to change it. The first time you use a language, it's downloaded to your device, so give it a moment. A few languages aren't supported yet, and the app says so.
Why can't I choose Cinematic?
The camera you're using doesn't support it. Most iPads don't, and some iPhone cameras don't either. Settings → Device Capabilities shows which cameras support it.
A long take stopped early
Check the warnings in the recorder. When space is low, the app shows how much is free and long takes may stop early. A hot device can also stop or drop quality. Free up some space, let the device cool, or record shorter takes.
Where did my take go?
Every take stays under Takes until you delete it there, even if you never saved it. Deleting it there never touches the copies you saved to Photos or Files.
