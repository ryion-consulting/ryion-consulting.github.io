The questions asked most

Some days are missing, or a trip wasn't recorded
The app needs Always location access. With While Using, nothing is recorded while the app is closed and those days show as gaps. In your device's Settings app, open Whereabouts Compass → Location and choose Always. Turning Location Services off has the same effect, and if Background App Refresh is off, logging can be delayed. To fill in days that were missed, open the trip and set its dates, or use Settings → Import from Photos.
A day shows "Not counted in any country"
There were no readings for that day. The phone may have been off or out of signal, or Location Services was off. If you know where you were, edit the trip next to the gap and set the right dates.
A border date has an amber ?
It's an estimate, because readings around that crossing were sparse. Open the trip and confirm or correct the dates. Any edit confirms the trip, so the app never changes it again.
iCloud sync isn't working
Turning Settings → Sync with iCloud on or off takes effect the next time the app opens, so close and reopen it. Then check Settings → Sync details, which says what is wrong, for example that you aren't signed in to iCloud or that your iCloud storage is full.
Import from Photos found nothing
Photos only help if they have a place stored with them. That doesn't happen when Location Services was off for the Camera, or for photos from a camera without GPS. With Limited Access, choose Share More Photos on the import screen to let it see more. You can always add trips by hand.
