Privacy Policy

The short version

Whereabouts Compass collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Your travel history is stored on your devices and, if iCloud sync is on, in your own private iCloud, which the developer cannot access. The app contacts Apple Maps for two things only: naming a city, and drawing the map on a trip's page.

What is collected

Nothing. The developer receives no location, no travel history, no usage data, no device identifiers and no crash reports. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

Location

The app's purpose is to record which country and city you are in, so it needs your location. It uses the visit and significant-location-change services built into iOS, which rely on cell and Wi-Fi rather than continuous GPS. Always permission lets it record a border you cross while the app is closed. The readings, the trips built from them and your edits are stored on your device, and in your private iCloud if sync is on.

The country is worked out on your device from maps bundled with the app, with no network request.

Network requests

The app makes two kinds of request, both to Apple and to no one else:

  • City names. To put a city name on a stay, the app sends that place's coordinate to Apple Maps.
  • Trip maps. When you open a trip, Apple Maps draws the small map on its page.

Both are covered by Apple's privacy policy. The app works fully if they never succeed. A stay just has no city name.

Photos

The photo import is optional. If you run it, the app reads only the date and location stored with each photo, plus an identifier so the same photo is not imported twice. It never opens, copies or uploads a picture. Limited Access is supported if you prefer to share only some photos. The import itself uses no network.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default, so your iPhone and iPad show the same history. Your data syncs through Apple's CloudKit to the private database of your own Apple Account. Only your Apple Account can read it. The developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off in Settings.

Exports and backups

PDF, CSV, text and backup files are created only when you ask, and go wherever you choose to share them. A full backup contains your complete location history, so keep it somewhere you trust. The app never uploads them.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no third-party libraries or software development kits. The only outside services it uses are Apple's iCloud and Apple Maps.

Children

Whereabouts Compass is not directed at children and knowingly collects no information from anyone.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Raw location readings are kept for the number of years set in Settings → Keep raw locations (10 by default). The trips built from them are kept after that.
  • Deleting a trip removes it from your list. Your recorded locations are kept, and you can restore the trip from Settings → Deleted Trips.
  • Settings → Privacy → Erase All Data deletes every reading, trip and setting, from your other devices too if sync is on.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. To remove the synced copy, use your device's iCloud storage settings.
  • Because nothing is collected, there is no account to close and nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Not advice

Whereabouts Compass counts days from your own data. It does not determine tax residency or immigration eligibility, and dates rebuilt from photos are evidence, not proof.
