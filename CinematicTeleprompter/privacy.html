Privacy Policy

The short version

Cinematic Teleprompter collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Your videos stay on your device until you choose to save or share them. Your scripts sync only through your own iCloud, which the developer cannot access. Speech for Voice-Follow is recognized on your device.

What is collected

Nothing. The developer receives no videos, scripts, audio, usage data, device identifiers or crash reports.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only while the recorder is open, to show the picture and record your takes. Recordings are stored in the app's own storage on your device and appear in the Takes list. They leave the app only when you tap Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share, and then go only where you send them. The app never uploads them, and they are not synced through iCloud.

Speech recognition

Voice-Follow listens while you read so it can scroll the script with you. It uses Apple's speech recognition on your device, and your audio isn't sent anywhere. The first time you use a language, iOS may download its speech model from Apple. That download contains no audio or text of yours.

Photos

The app asks only for permission to add to your Photos library, used when you tap Save to Photos. It cannot see the photos and videos already in your library.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your scripts sync through Apple's CloudKit to the private database of your own Apple Account, so they appear on your iPhone and iPad. A few shared preferences also sync through your iCloud: scroll speed and mode, the countdown, saved-video options and the default language. Only your Apple Account can read this data. The developer has no access to it. Text layout, camera settings and recorded takes stay on each device. Without iCloud, everything works on the one device.

Purchases

The Yearly and Monthly plans, the free trial and Lifetime Unlock are all handled by Apple through the App Store. The app sees only which of them your Apple Account has, when a plan renews or ends, and whether the free trial has been used. It never sees your payment details.

Notifications

When you start the free trial, the app asks whether it may remind you 2 days before the trial ends. The reminder is scheduled on your device and shown by iOS. Nothing is sent anywhere to make it appear.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no third-party libraries or software development kits and runs no server of its own. The only outside services it uses are Apple's: iCloud, the App Store, and speech model downloads. These are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Cinematic Teleprompter is not directed at children and knowingly collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your information

  • Delete a take from the Takes list to remove it from the app. Copies you saved to Photos or Files are not affected.
  • Delete a script and it is removed from this device and, with sync, from your other devices.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device, including takes you did not save.
  • To remove the synced copy of your scripts, use your device's iCloud storage settings.
  • Because nothing is collected, there is no account to close and nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
